Recipe: Stuffed Shells

HOME

Jumbo stuffed pasta shells filled with cheese mixture, covered with sauce, and baked.

A classic stuffed shells recipe layers jumbo pasta shells with a creamy ricotta and spinach filling, tucks them into a rich marinara sauce, then sprinkles mozzarella and Parmesan on top before baking until bubbly and golden — the result is a comforting, crowd-pleasing dish that combines tender pasta, cheesy filling, and savory tomato flavor in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 12 jumbo pasta shells
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh spinach
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

Step 1

Cook the jumbo pasta shells according to the package instructions until al dente, then drain and set aside.

Step 2

Mix the ricotta, Parmesan, spinach, egg, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper in a bowl until well combined.

Step 3

Spread a thin layer of marinara sauce in a baking dish.

Step 4

Fill each cooked pasta shell with the cheese mixture and place them in the dish.

Step 5

Spoon the remaining marinara sauce over the shells.

Step 6

Sprinkle mozzarella and a little extra Parmesan on top.

Step 7

Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F until bubbly and golden, about 25–30 minutes.

Step 8

Let the stuffed shells cool slightly before serving.
